# topic links the question to a disease or drug id (or "law").

=== mcq: m1
topic: malaria-uncomplicated
q: A 4-year-old (16 kg) has fever and a positive mRDT with no danger signs. What is the correct artemether-lumefantrine dose?
a: 2 tablets (20/120 mg) per dose, 6 doses over 3 days
w: 1 tablet per dose, 6 doses over 3 days | 3 tablets per dose, 6 doses over 3 days | 2 tablets once daily for 3 days
why: 15–24 kg = 2 tablets per dose at 0, 8, 24, 36, 48 and 60 hours, taken with fatty food or milk.
